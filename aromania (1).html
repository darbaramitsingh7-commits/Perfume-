OUR BEGINNING

AROMANIA started on a kitchen table with a handful of absolutes, a notebook of failed formulas, and the belief that a fragrance could be composed the way a room is furnished — one considered note at a time.

We still work the same way. No note goes into a bottle until it has earned its place beside the ones already there.
